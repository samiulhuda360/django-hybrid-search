Accounts

Single sign-on

Business plan teams can require members to sign in through their company's identity provider.

Setting it up

Single sign-on supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. Enter the details from your identity provider under Team settings, Security, then test the connection with your own account before turning it on for everyone.

Enforcing sign-on

Once enforced, members must use the identity provider and their passwords no longer work. Owners keep a recovery code in case the identity provider is down.

Automatic accounts

New users who sign in through the identity provider can be added to the team automatically with the Developer role.
