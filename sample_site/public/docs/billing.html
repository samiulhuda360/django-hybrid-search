Accounts

Billing and invoices

Billing is handled per team.

Plans

The Starter plan is free and suited to hobby projects. The Pro plan adds autoscaling, longer log retention and point-in-time database recovery. The Business plan adds single sign-on, several regions and your own certificates.

Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month and emailed to the billing contact. Download past invoices as PDF from Team settings, Billing. You can add a tax number and company address to future invoices.

Payment methods

Pay by card on every plan. Business plan customers can also pay by bank transfer against an annual invoice. If a payment fails, we retry for 14 days before projects are paused.
