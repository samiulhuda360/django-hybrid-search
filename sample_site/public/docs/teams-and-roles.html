Teams and roles

Teams let several people work on the same projects with different permissions.

Roles

Owners manage billing and can delete the team. Admins manage members, domains and secrets. Developers can deploy, view logs and change variables. Viewers can only look at dashboards and logs.

Inviting people

Open Team settings, Members and invite by email address. The invitation is valid for 7 days. Each team needs at least one owner.

Removing people

Removing a member ends their sessions at once and revokes their API tokens for this team.
