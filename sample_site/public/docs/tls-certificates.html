Networking

HTTPS and TLS certificates

Every app is served over HTTPS. Certificates are issued and renewed automatically, at no cost.

Automatic certificates

After a custom domain's DNS records are correct, a certificate is issued within a few minutes. It is renewed 30 days before it expires, with no action from you.

Bringing your own certificate

On the Business plan you can upload your own certificate and private key with acme certs add. You are then responsible for renewing it before it expires.

Redirecting HTTP

Plain HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS by default. You can also turn on HSTS in the domain settings so browsers always use the secure address.
