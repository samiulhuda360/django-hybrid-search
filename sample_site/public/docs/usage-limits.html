Accounts

Plan limits and usage

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of instance hours, bandwidth and build minutes.

Checking usage

The Usage page shows how much of each allowance the team has used this month and a forecast for the full month.

Going over the allowance

On the Starter plan, apps are paused when an allowance runs out and start again at the beginning of the next month. On paid plans extra usage is charged at the rates on the pricing page, and you can set a spending cap.

Spending caps

A spending cap stops extra charges above an amount you choose. When the cap is reached, preview environments stop first, then the remaining apps scale down to one instance.
